Tax deadlines

Never miss an HMRC deadline

Key dates for self-employed people, CIS subcontractors and Making Tax Digital. Add them to your calendar to get a reminder.

Next deadline: ,

  1. 5 Oct 2026

    Register for Self Assessment

    If you started working for yourself (including under CIS) in the 2025/26 tax year, you must register with HMRC by this date.

  2. 31 Oct 2026

    2025/26 paper tax return

    Last day for paper Self Assessment returns. Online returns are due by 31 January.

  3. 7 Nov 2026

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 July – 5 October 2026, if you're in Making Tax Digital (income over £50,000).

  4. 30 Dec 2026

    Pay through your PAYE tax code

    If you're also employed and owe less than £3,000, file online by today to have it collected through your wages.

  5. 31 Jan 2027

    2025/26 online tax return and payment

    Final deadline to file online and pay your tax, plus your first payment on account for 2026/27. Filing late means a £100 penalty.

  6. 7 Feb 2027

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 October 2026 – 5 January 2027.

  7. 5 Apr 2027

    End of the 2026/27 tax year

    The tax year closes. Start gathering your records for the next return.

  8. 7 May 2027

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 January – 5 April 2027.

  9. 31 Jul 2027

    Second payment on account for 2026/27

    Your second payment on account, if HMRC asked you to make them.

What documents to send us

The documents we need, depending on your situation. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

CIS and self-employed

  • Your UTR and National Insurance number

Limited company

  • Company bank statements, monthly
  • Sales invoices
  • Purchase invoices and receipts

  • Company UTR and VAT number, if you have one

  • For each director: name, date of birth, nationality, address

  • Valid photo ID for Companies House identity verification
  • What the company does
  • Shareholders and number of shares

FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

What is a UTR and do I need one?

A UTR (Unique Taxpayer Reference) is a 10-digit number from HMRC. You need one if you are self-employed or have to file a Self Assessment tax return. We can register you.

When is the Self Assessment deadline?

The UK tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April the following year. Online returns must be filed by 31 January after the tax year ends. HMRC charges a £100 penalty for late filing, which increases over time.

I work in construction under CIS. Can I get money back?

In many cases, yes. CIS payments usually have 20% deducted. Once allowable expenses are claimed (tools, travel, protective equipment and so on), many clients get part of that money back.

What is Making Tax Digital and does it affect me?

Making Tax Digital (MTD) means keeping digital records and sending quarterly updates to HMRC through compatible software. It already applies to all VAT-registered businesses. Since April 2026 it also applies to self-employed people and landlords with income over £50,000, and the threshold falls in the coming years.

Should I be self-employed or set up a limited company?

It depends on how much you earn and the type of work you do. We look at your situation and tell you which option works best for you.

I received a letter from HMRC and don't understand it. Can you help?

Yes. Send us a photo of the letter on WhatsApp and we'll explain what it means and what needs to be done.
